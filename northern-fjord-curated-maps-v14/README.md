# Skyhorn — Curated Additive Maps

Internal ID: `northern-fjord-curated-maps-v14`

Target: Foundry VTT v14.

This module does not contain or replace the campaign's existing 14 bundled Skyhorn/Northern Fjord scenes. It adds a curated importer for relevant scenes supplied by installed map-source modules.

## Curated categories

- Harbor & Docks
- Taverns & Inns
- Lighthouse & Towers
- Coast, Roads & Passes
- Caves & Underwater
- Village & Town
- Ruins & Shrines
- Winter & Wilderness
- Guards & Smugglers

These categories cover the current campaign plus the previously requested optional expansion locations: fjord roads, fishing huts/outskirts, sea caves/smuggler hideouts, watchtower ruins, winter-storm terrain, underwater/eelfolk encounter space, tide pools/shoreline, town guard/barracks, old shrine/relic sites, and ice-field/smuggler-camp encounters.

## Safety

Imports are additive. Existing world scenes are not deleted or updated. Imported scenes store their source Scene UUID under module flags and are skipped on later runs.
